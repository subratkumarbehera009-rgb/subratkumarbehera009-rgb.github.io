My research bridges finite-strain continuum mechanics, multiphysics coupling, and nonlinear dynamics of smart soft materials. I develop micromechanics-based constitutive frameworks and physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for field-responsive polymers, multiferroic soft solids, magnetorheological fluids, and 3D-printed architected metamaterials for energy absorption and battery abuse protection.

Research Pillars & Thrusts

Integrating fundamental physics, continuum mechanics, and machine learning to engineer smart materials and structural systems.

🧲

Multiphysics of Soft Active & Multiferroic Solids

Investigating coupled magneto-electro-mechanical loading, finite viscoelasticity, active deformation, and growth in multiferroic elastomers. Formulating micromechanics-based finite-deformation theories and stability criteria under complex physical stimuli.

Electro-Magneto-Elasticity Active Growth Multiferroics Composites Sci & Tech
⚙️

Theoretical & Nonlinear Continuum Mechanics

Developing finite-strain kinematics, objective rate formulations, damage-induced stress softening (Mullins effect), and universal rate-dependent constitutive laws for polymeric and particle-reinforced composites.

Finite Viscoelasticity Hyperelasticity Damage Mechanics IJSS & EJMAS
📈

Nonlinear Dynamics, Bifurcation & Chaos Control

Analyzing periodic forcing, bistability, and chaotic oscillations in dielectric elastomer energy generators (DEGs), artificial muscles, and biomimetic circular membranes, with targeted control techniques for vibration mitigation and energy harvesting.

Dielectric Elastomers Chaos Control Bifurcation JSV & CNSNS
🧠

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)

Designing thermodynamically consistent machine learning frameworks that strictly obey the laws of continuum mechanics (second law of thermodynamics, frame indifference) to learn complex constitutive responses of active soft polymers from sparse experimental data.

PINNs Data-Driven Mechanics Thermodynamic Consistency VHB 4905
🔋

Architected Metamaterials & Battery Protection

Pioneering rate-dependent, large-deformation constitutive models for 3D-printable architected metamaterials and polymeric battery separators under dynamic crash/mechanical-abuse conditions to prevent thermal runaway in next-generation electric vehicles.

Battery Separators 3D Printing NSF CAREER NSF I-Corps 2024
💧

Smart Rheology & Magnetorheological Fluids

Developing unified continuum models capturing pre-yield viscoelasticity, post-yield flow, and sign-reversal phenomena in magnetorheological (MR) fluids for smart brake and damper applications.

MR Fluids Physics of Fluids Smart Brakes Mechanics Res Comm
